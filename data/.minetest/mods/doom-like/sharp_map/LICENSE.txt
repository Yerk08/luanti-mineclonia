The mod ships no Mineclonia textures or data. At runtime it reads
Mineclonia's map colors (mcl_maps/colors.json) from the installed game and
refers to Mineclonia's map item texture (mcl_maps_map_filled.png) and
button textures (mcl_inventory_button9*.png) by name.
