The mod ships no Mineclonia textures. At runtime it shows its own
textures in place of Mineclonia's GUI textures. The creative Map button
(shown only with the Sharp Map mod) uses Mineclonia's own map item
texture (mcl_maps_map_filled.png) from the installed game; it is not
included in this mod.
